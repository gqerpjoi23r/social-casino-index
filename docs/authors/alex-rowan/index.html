How the work is done

Research on this site follows the published methodology: accounts are funded with the site's own money, redemptions are requested as an ordinary player, and each stage is timed from request to funds received. Where a figure comes from an operator's published terms rather than a completed funded test, it is labeled as such. The site does not invent credentials, employers, or personal testing history; figures that have not been measured are marked pending or unverified.

Every article is reviewed before publication. Material corrections are recorded with a date in the article's change log when one is present, and the "last updated" date reflects the most recent material review. Articles are re-checked as new operator research is completed.
