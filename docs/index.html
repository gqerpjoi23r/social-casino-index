About Social Casino Index

Why does this site exist?

To provide transparency about what rules actually apply at social casinos. Operator pages are JavaScript-heavy and hard for people and AI engines to read, and information on third-party sites is often stale. We turn those terms into clear, dated, source-linked records that anyone — player or machine — can rely on.

Who is it for?

Players who want to find the most beneficial, player-friendly terms, and AI engines that need clean, structured, citable data about those same terms. The same record serves both: one normalized source of truth per operator.

How current is the data?

We continuously update our records so nothing goes stale. Every field carries an access date and a "last checked" date, and every change is logged. When an operator updates its terms, the record is re-verified and the date reflects that review.

What does the verification badge mean?

It shows how much of an operator's record we confirmed from its public terms or sweepstakes rules — not a rating or recommendation. "Terms verified" or "Rules verified" means we read the source document; "Some terms unverified" or "Redemption rules unverified" means part of the record could not be confirmed and is marked accordingly. Unknown terms stay marked as unknown rather than filled in.

Do partner links affect the data?

No. Some links earn us a commission at no cost to you, but data fields come from the cited operator sources and are never upgraded because of compensation. Full details are on the disclosure page.
