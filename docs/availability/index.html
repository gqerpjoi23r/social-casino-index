Closed states

The following states are treated as closed on this site, and commercial offers are blocked for them. This list reflects operator availability policy, not legal advice; state law in this category changes frequently.

  • Closed by statute. California, New York, Connecticut, Indiana, Louisiana, Maine, Montana, Nevada, New Jersey, Oklahoma, Tennessee.
  • Closed by enforcement. Idaho, Michigan, Washington. No sweepstakes-specific law, but regulators apply existing gambling statutes.

California is the most significant recent change: AB 831 was signed on 11 October 2025 and took effect on 1 January 2026.

Open states

Most other states, including Texas, Florida, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Georgia and North Carolina, are treated as open. Individual operators may still choose not to serve specific states; the operator's own terms govern.
