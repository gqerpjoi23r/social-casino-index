How a bank-transfer redemption works

  • Verification first. First redemptions require identity and address checks before the operator releases funds.
  • Account-name match. The receiving account must be in your name; mismatches are a common cause of rejection.
  • Banking days only. Delivery settles on banking days, so a Friday approval can land the following week.
  • Caps can apply. Check the current redemption rules for per-request or daily limits.

Bank transfer vs other rails

A bank transfer avoids cryptocurrency wallet handling. Gift cards can have lower minimums but are not unrestricted cash. We have not established a fastest method through comparable funded tests. Compare the records in the redemption methods guide.
