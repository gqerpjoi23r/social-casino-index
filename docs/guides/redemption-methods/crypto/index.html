How a crypto redemption works

  • Separate the stages. Network settlement begins after the operator releases a redemption.
  • Verification first. Check the operator's identity requirements and approval status before expecting delivery.
  • Wallet handling. A wrong address or network can lose the redemption with no bank-style recall.
  • Asset-value risk. The value of the received asset can change between approval and when you convert it.

Crypto vs other rails

A bank transfer avoids wallet handling, while gift cards can have lower minimums but are not cash. Compare method-specific conditions in the redemption methods guide and the redemption-time research.
