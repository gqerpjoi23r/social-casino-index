Method matters as much as the headline time

An operator can approve a request quickly while the payment rail adds more time. Compare the full path: identity review, operator processing and final delivery. A published “processing” estimate is not necessarily the time until funds arrive.

Minimums can change the best option

Method-specific thresholds matter for smaller balances. McLuck publishes different minimums for bank transfer and gift cards, while Stake.us publishes a 40 SC minimum and Dorados publishes 100 SC. Always confirm the live cashier before playing toward a target.
