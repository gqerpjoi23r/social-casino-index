Sweepstakes Casino Taxes and Prize Records

Keep complete records of prizes, redemptions and account activity. Tax treatment depends on your facts and jurisdiction, so use these records with a qualified tax professional.

Practical takeaway

Do not wait for tax season to reconstruct account history. Export statements when available and log each redemption with its request date, received date, value, method and supporting document.

Records to keep

  • Redemption log. Operator, amount, request date, approval date, received date and method.
  • Account statements. Save downloadable transaction and play histories before an account closes or a retention window expires.
  • Payment evidence. Keep bank, gift-card or wallet records that match each redemption.
  • Operator forms. Retain any tax forms or year-end summaries and note discrepancies promptly.
  • Supporting costs. Keep records separately and ask a tax professional which items, if any, apply to your situation.
