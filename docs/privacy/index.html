Privacy Policy

What we collect

This site does not have accounts, forms that submit personal data to us, or a comments system. We do not collect names, email addresses, payment details or identity documents.

The state cookie

If you select a state in the availability picker, the two-letter state code is stored in a cookie (sci_state) on your device for 30 days. It is used only to decide whether commercial offers are shown to you. The selection is not transmitted to our servers by that cookie beyond normal page requests, and you can clear it at any time through your browser.

Analytics

Analytics (Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4, under business-owned accounts) is being configured and will operate behind a consent banner (Cookiebot). The events recorded are limited to: state_selected, article_view, comparison_interaction, source_click, methodology_click, affiliate_click_attempt, affiliate_click_allowed and affiliate_click_blocked. No evidence, account, payment, KYC or other personal data is sent to analytics.

Affiliate redirects

Links under /go/ redirect to operator sites. The operator you land on has its own privacy policy, which governs any data you give them.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard request data (IP address, user agent) per its own privacy statement.
