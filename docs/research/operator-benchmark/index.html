How we verify

Each operator's terms and sweepstakes rules are fetched directly, or via a US-located scrape when the operator blocks our region. Fields we cannot confirm from a public document are marked unverified — never filled in from assumption. Verification access dates are recorded in the dataset and updated on re-check.
