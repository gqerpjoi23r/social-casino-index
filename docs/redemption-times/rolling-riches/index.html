Rolling Riches redemption status meanings

Pending

The request is submitted and waiting for review. Identity verification is often completed at this stage.

In progress or processing

The operator is reviewing or preparing the payment. Some operators use these labels for the same stage as pending.

Approved, scheduled or processed

The operator has accepted the request and sent it, or will send it, to the payment provider. Delivery time then depends on the method. Rolling Riches publishes delivery after approval of 24-48 hours for debit card.

Status names vary between operators. Your account redemption history shows the exact label.
