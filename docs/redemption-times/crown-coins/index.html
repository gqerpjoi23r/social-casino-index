Crown Coins redemption status meanings

Pending

The request is submitted and waiting for review. Identity verification is often completed at this stage. Crown Coins publishes approval of 24-72 hours.

In progress or processing

The operator is reviewing or preparing the payment. Some operators use these labels for the same stage as pending.

Approved, scheduled or processed

The operator has accepted the request and sent it, or will send it, to the payment provider. Delivery time then depends on the method. Crown Coins publishes delivery after approval of 1-3 business days and typically 3 business days.

Status names vary between operators. Your account redemption history shows the exact label.
