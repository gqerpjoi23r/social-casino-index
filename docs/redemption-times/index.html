What the data shows

Published payout language is not directly comparable unless you separate approval, processing and delivery. Chumba publishes processing within two days after approval, Pulsz publishes 3-7 business days for a first redemption, and McLuck publishes 2-5 days for bank delivery after internal processing. Several newer operators do not publish a complete timeframe.
