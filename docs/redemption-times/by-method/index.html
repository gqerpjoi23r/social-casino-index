Questions about payout methods

Which sweepstakes casino payment method is fastest?

Social Casino Index does not name a fastest method yet. The published windows above cover different stages, such as processing, approval or delivery after approval, and funded tests are still needed for a measured comparison.

Are gift-card redemptions faster than bank transfers?

Some operators publish shorter gift-card windows than bank-transfer windows, but the stages are not always the same. Gift-card minimums can also be lower than cash minimums.

Why do first redemptions take longer?

First redemptions usually require identity and bank or payment-account verification before the operator releases a prize.
