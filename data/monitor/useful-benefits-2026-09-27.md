# Useful benefits, same list and sorts

## Product decision

List an operator when at least two useful, source-backed benefit areas can be
shown. Areas: welcome/signup, purchase offer, daily reward, redemption timing,
and redemption minimum. Cash and gift minimums are one area. Concrete text
counts; generic praise and empty rows do not.

Keep the homepage layout and its five numeric sorts. Default remains immediate
free signup SC, unknowns last, ties alphabetical. Descriptions, entertainment
credits, FC and gift-card minimums do not acquire invented comparable values.
Delivery after approval can be useful without being a processing-speed result.

## Benchmark research

Rechecked September 27, 2026:

- https://sweepskings.com/ leads with welcome/purchase offers and mixes them
  with method, minimum, game and qualitative highlights. Daily rewards can be
  advertised as an upper limit rather than a fixed amount.
- https://sweepcasinos.com/ combines welcome offers with short distinct benefits:
  gift redemption, payout windows, daily wheels, payment methods and game range.
  It displays non-SC denominations as such in some offers.

This supports separating useful display information from sortable numbers.
It does not prove which claims are true or how many players value each one.
The existing September 26 attribute research and US keyword study remain the
demand evidence; competitor coverage is a presentation benchmark, not a survey.

SweepCasinos advertises SC amounts for Jackpota. Official checks of
https://www.jackpota.com/ and https://www.jackpota.com/terms-of-service instead
describe ordinary entertainment credits, while reserving separate promotional
contests. Do not import competitor amounts or exclude all Jackpota benefits.
The official promotions page was a loading shell in a direct check and timed
out waiting for browser network-idle. It remains a normal monitored source,
now owned by the registry rather than duplicated in collection configuration.

## Scraper concept

Selected official pages and useful PDFs -> direct retrieval with existing
Firecrawl fallback -> private text/evidence archive -> structured extraction
of amounts and descriptions -> source checks -> one shared display model.
Only comparable numbers enter the existing sorts. No new service, crawler,
parallel value store or configured request limits.
